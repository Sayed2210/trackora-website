## The Problem with Fake Orders

Shipping companies face different types of fraud:

- **Orders with invalid phone numbers**: The customer never picks up and the courier wastes a trip.
- **Weak addresses**: Unclear or nonexistent addresses — the shipment bounces and gets returned at high cost.
- **Repeated refusal history**: A customer who keeps refusing delivery — each time the merchant pays shipping and return fees.

The result? Lost money, wasted time, and courier effort that could have served real customers.

## Invalid Phone Numbers

Fraud Detection in Trackora automatically verifies the phone number:

- **Format check**: Is the number in the correct format for the country?
- **Active check**: Has this number been used before?
- **History check**: Has this number been associated with refused shipments before?

If the number doesn't pass verification, the system flags the order for review before it moves.

## Weak Addresses

Trackora analyzes the address and determines:

1. Is the address in a zone the company serves?
2. Is the address complete and detailed enough?
3. Have past shipments been successfully delivered to this address?

If the address is insufficient, the system requests confirmation before releasing the shipment to the courier.

## Repeated Failure History

This is the most dangerous type of fraud — a customer who orders, receives, and refuses time after time. Trackora:

- **Records every delivery attempt** for this customer.
- **Calculates the refusal rate** compared to total orders.
- **Alerts automatically** when the rate exceeds a threshold.
- **Suggests actions** — such as requiring prepayment or canceling shipments for this customer.

## Saving Courier Time and Effort

When fraud drops, couriers work on real shipments only. This means:

- **Fewer wasted trips** — couriers don't go to nonexistent addresses.
- **Higher delivery rates** — shipments that move are shipments that actually get delivered.
- **Lower costs** — no return fees for fake orders.
