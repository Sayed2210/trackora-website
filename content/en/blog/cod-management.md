## The Real Cost of COD Collection Problems

Cash on Delivery is the most common payment method in the Arab region, but it comes with serious challenges:

- **Delayed settlements**: Cash can sit with couriers for days before reaching the merchant.
- **Counting errors**: Manual cash counting opens the door to mistakes and discrepancies.
- **Hard to track**: Which courier collected how much? Which merchant received what? Who still owes what?

## Tracking Cash with Couriers

Trackora tracks every cash transaction in real time:

- **Payment registration**: The courier records payment upon delivery.
- **Live balance**: You always know exactly how much each courier is holding.
- **Alerts**: If no collection is recorded after a set time, the system notifies you.

## Merchant Wallet

Every merchant gets a digital wallet in Trackora:

- **Clear balance**: The merchant sees collected, pending, and rejected amounts.
- **Instant notifications**: Alerts when a shipment is paid and when funds are transferred.
- **Full history**: Every transaction has a date and reference number.

## Settlements

Instead of spreadsheets and manual reviews:

- **Automatic settlement**: Funds are transferred to the merchant's account on a clear schedule.
- **Detailed reports**: Every settlement includes a breakdown of shipments and amounts.
- **Full transparency**: The merchant sees what's owed before it arrives.

## Reducing Accounting Errors

When everything is recorded digitally and tracked, accounting errors drop significantly. No numbers get lost, and no settlement goes undocumented.
