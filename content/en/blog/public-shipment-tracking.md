## Fewer Support Calls

The most common customer question: "Where is my shipment?"

Without a clear way to track, customers call, message, and overwhelm support. A public tracking page lets customers check their shipment status on their own — and support call volume drops significantly.

## Showing Shipment Status to the Customer

Trackora's tracking page displays:

- **Current status** — Pending, Out for Delivery, Delivered...
- **Shipment details** — tracking number, merchant name, estimated delivery.
- **Customer info** — name and masked phone number for privacy.

All in the customer's language — Arabic or English.

## A Clear Timeline

Instead of showing just one status, a timeline tells the full story:

1. Shipment received from merchant.
2. Picked up by courier.
3. Arrived at distribution center.
4. Out for final delivery.
5. Delivered.

Each stage has a timestamp and clear details — the customer knows exactly what happened and what's next.

## Easy Tracking Number

Customers can either enter the tracking number directly or open the link sent by the merchant. No login required — it's a public, open page for anyone with a tracking number.

## A Professional Experience for Merchants and Shipping Companies

The tracking page doesn't just help customers — it helps the business:

- **Professional brand**: The tracking link appears in the customer's message — the merchant looks organized.
- **Less pressure**: Support focuses on real problems, not status inquiries.
- **Better reputation**: A smooth experience builds trust and repeat purchases.
