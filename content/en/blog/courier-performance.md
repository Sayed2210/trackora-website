## Why Measure Courier Performance?

The courier is the face of your company to the customer. Their performance directly affects customer satisfaction and your brand reputation. Without measurement, you can't identify who's doing well and who needs improvement.

## Measuring Delivery Success Rate

The most important metric: **the percentage of shipments delivered successfully** out of total shipments assigned to the courier.

- An ideal rate is above 95%.
- Below 90% signals a problem — whether with the zone or the courier's performance.
- Trackora displays this rate for every courier in the dashboard.

## Tracking Delays

Delays directly affect the customer experience. Trackora helps you monitor:

1. **Average delivery time** per courier, from pickup to delivery.
2. **Number of delayed shipments** compared to on-time ones.
3. **Peak days** when a specific courier is more likely to be late.

## Daily Shipment Volume

How many shipments does a courier deliver per day? This number varies by zone and service type, but comparing couriers in the same zone gives a clear picture of each person's productivity.

## Update Quality

Couriers must update shipment status in real time — picked up, in transit, delivered. Trackora tracks:

- **On-time update rate** per courier.
- **Missing updates** that the courier forgot to log.
- **Customer notes** linked to courier performance.

## Improving Work Distribution

When you know each courier's performance, you can:

- Assign more shipments to higher performers.
- Identify couriers who need additional training.
- Reward top performers and keep them motivated.
