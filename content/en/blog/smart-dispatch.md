## The Problem with Manual Sorting

Most shipping companies still distribute shipments by hand — someone reads the address, determines the zone, then assigns a courier. This process takes hours every day and increases the likelihood of human error, especially at high volume.

## Zone-Based Distribution

Smart Dispatch solves this the moment a shipment enters the system:

- **Auto-classification**: The shipment is categorized by geographic zone instantly.
- **Smart grouping**: Shipments in the same zone are batched together so the courier picks them up in a single trip.
- **Live updates**: If a shipment's zone changes, the system re-assigns it automatically.

## Choosing the Best Courier

Smart Dispatch doesn't just pick a zone — it picks the right courier based on:

1. **Current location** of the courier.
2. **Active load** — how many shipments they're already carrying.
3. **Delivery success rate** in that specific zone.

The result? Every shipment reaches the nearest, most efficient courier instead of being distributed at random.

## Reducing Delays

When sorting is manual, shipments sit in a queue waiting to be processed. Smart Dispatch completes distribution in seconds, so the shipment starts moving from the very first moment — cutting total delivery time noticeably.

## Improving Operational Efficiency

When manual sorting is eliminated, the team can focus on higher-value tasks:
- Handling exceptions and escalations.
- Following up on delayed shipments.
- Improving service quality overall.
